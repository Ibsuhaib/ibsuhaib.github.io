Privacy Policy for Skitter's Escape

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Skitter's Escape ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android made by Ibsuhaib ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the game uses.

What stays on your phone

Your level, coins, scores, chosen suit and sound settings are saved only on your device. I don't run accounts or servers that store them, and I can't see them.

Ads

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure results and prevent fraud. Where required, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later in the game's privacy options if shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's own policy explains how it handles this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Game updates

When you open the game, it may check a public update page hosted on GitHub Pages for newer game files. Like any website request, this lets GitHub see your IP address. I don't receive or store any information about you from this check.

What I don't collect

The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone. I don't sell personal information.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its date.
